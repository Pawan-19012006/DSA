# Data Structures & Algorithms (Java)

A structured collection of **data structures, algorithms,
problem-solving approaches, and important programming concepts**,
implemented in Java.

This repository serves as my personal DSA learning and interview
preparation workspace, documenting concepts, implementation techniques,
and solutions to problems commonly encountered in technical interviews.

## Topics Covered

The repository is organized by topic to make learning, revision, and
navigation easier.

-   **Recursion & Backtracking** --- Recursion fundamentals, subsets,
    permutations, combination problems, N-Queens, and more.
-   **Arrays & Strings** --- Common patterns, manipulation techniques,
    and problem-solving approaches.
-   **Binary Search** --- Search techniques, variations, and
    optimization problems.
-   **Linked Lists** --- Traversal, manipulation, and common interview
    problems.
-   **Stacks & Queues** --- Fundamental operations and application-based
    problems.
-   **Trees & Binary Search Trees** --- Traversals, tree operations, and
    problem-solving patterns.
-   **Heaps & Priority Queues** --- Heap operations and selection-based
    problems.
-   **Graphs** --- Traversals, shortest paths, and graph algorithms.
-   **Dynamic Programming** --- Memoization, tabulation, and
    optimization problems.
-   **Greedy Algorithms** --- Greedy strategies and optimization
    problems.

*Topics will be added progressively as the repository evolves.*
